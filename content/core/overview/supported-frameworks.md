# Other frameworks and languages

The i18next team maintains [react-i18next](/react), [next-i18next](/next), [i18next-cli](/cli) and the [Astro](/astro) plugin, each with a docs section of its own, and the packages below.

## From the i18next team

* **Node.js servers (Express, Fastify) and Deno:** [i18next-http-middleware](https://github.com/i18next/i18next-http-middleware)
* **jQuery:** [jquery-i18next](https://github.com/i18next/jquery-i18next)
* **Any web page, without changing its code:** [i18nextify](https://github.com/i18next/i18nextify)

## From the community

The list holds projects with a change in the last twelve months, finished projects that work with the current i18next and are still used (marked as finished), and projects the maintainers keep (checked 2026-11-21).

* **Angular:** [angular-i18next](https://github.com/Romanchuk/angular-i18next)
* **Astro:** [@gutenye/astro-i18next](https://github.com/gutenye/astro-i18next). The i18next team's Astro path is [Astro](/astro); this community package is another way.
* **Handlebars:** [handlebars-i18n](https://github.com/Aller-Couleur/handlebars-i18n)
* **Koa:** [koa-i18next-middleware](https://github.com/lxzxl/koa-i18next-middleware)
* **Lit:** [lit-i18n](https://github.com/colscott/lit-i18n)
* **Remix:** [remix-i18next](https://github.com/sergiodxa/remix-i18next)
* **Svelte:** [svelte-i18next](https://github.com/NishuGoel/svelte-i18next)
* **Vue.js:** [i18next-vue](https://github.com/i18next/i18next-vue)

## Other programming languages

Ports of i18next to other languages, by the community.

* **.NET:** [I18Next.Net](https://github.com/DarkLiKally/I18Next.Net)
* **Flutter / Dart:** [i18next](https://pub.dev/packages/i18next)
* **Go:** [i18next\_go](https://pkg.go.dev/codeberg.org/lil5/i18next_go)
