## What happens when you visit

**Hosting.** The site is served by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) (Cloudflare, Inc.). To deliver the pages and protect the site against attacks, Cloudflare processes
your IP address, the requested address, the time, your browser's user agent and the referring page. We have a
legitimate interest in a working and secure site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare may process this data outside
Switzerland and the EU; it relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the Data Privacy Framework for this.

**Visitor statistics without cookies.** A small script from Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and measures how
fast pages load. It sends the page address, the referring page, your browser type, your country and loading times to
Cloudflare. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not identify you across sites. We have a
legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read and whether they load quickly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Search.** The search runs in your browser. It downloads index files from this site; your search words are not sent
anywhere.

**Settings in your browser.** The site stores two things in your browser, both needed for it to work as you set it:
your answer to the cookie banner (cookie `cc_cookie_i18next`, kept for six months) and, if you switch it, light or dark
mode (`theme` in local storage). The theme stays in your browser. The cookie goes along with each request to the site,
as cookies do, but the site does not read or store it.

## Analytics, only with your consent

If you accept analytics in the banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4 from Google Ireland Limited (Google LLC for
visitors outside the EEA). It tells us which pages are read and how visitors find them, so we know which parts of the
documentation help. Google Analytics sets cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_<id>`, up to two years) and receives your browser and
device details, the pages you view and an approximate location; it does not store IP addresses. Google may process the
data in the USA (EU Standard Contractual Clauses, Data Privacy Framework).

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you reject analytics, Google Analytics is not loaded. You can
change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page; withdrawing consent deletes the
analytics cookies and applies from then on. Details: [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can
withdraw a consent at any time. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your
country or the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). To exercise your rights, write to
privacy@inweso.ch.
